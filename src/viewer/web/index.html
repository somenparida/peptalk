Navigation & Controls Guide

Welcome to the 3D Architectural Scene Viewer. Use these straightforward controls to explore the building:

Mouse Navigation (Orbit Mode)
Rotate View Left Click and hold, then drag mouse in any direction.
Pan Camera Right Click (or Shift + Left Click) and drag to move sideways.
Zoom In / Out Scroll mouse wheel up or down.
Inspect a Room Click any room name in the right sidebar to fly straight inside it.
First-Person Walkthrough
Start Walking Click the "Walkthrough" button in the top bar.
Movement Use W (Forward), A (Left), S (Backward), D (Right).
Look Around Move your mouse to look in full 360 degrees at eye level (1.6m).
Exit Walk Press ESC to unlock mouse and return to Orbit mode.
Measurement Ruler
Measure Distance Click "Measure Ruler" on the left toolbar, then click point A and point B on any wall or floor to view exact distance in meters.
